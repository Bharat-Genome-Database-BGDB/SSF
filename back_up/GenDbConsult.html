Enabling Modern Genomic Research

At the Sivasakthi Science Foundation, we provide tailored support to help organizations build, scale, and manage their own genome databases. Our expert team offers solutions that address the unique challenges of genomic data—from small research projects to large-scale, public repositories.

Design & Architecture

Guiding organizations through implementing robust infrastructure for multi-dimensional genomic datasets.

  • Custom Architecture Design (Relational/NoSQL)
  • Software Platform Evaluation
  • Scalability Roadmaps

Curation & Management

Developing best practices for data accuracy, usability, and accessibility for downstream analysis.

  • Data Cleaning & Validation Protocols
  • FAIR Principle Compliance
  • Metadata Standardization
  • Data Versioning for Reproducibility

Optimization & Tuning

Tuning databases for speed and reliability, especially for next-generation sequencing (NGS) demands.

  • High-Availability Mechanisms

  • Advanced Security & GDPR Compliance

  • Auto-scaling Configurations

Capacity Building

Empowering teams with the knowledge to maintain genome databases effectively over the long term.

  • Hands-on Workshops
  • Custom Training Modules
  • Bioinformatics Tool Proficiency
  • Ongoing Technical Support

Open Science Support
