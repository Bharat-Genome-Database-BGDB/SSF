Programs & Initiatives

Creating opportunities for learning, discovery, and collaboration that benefit the scientific community and society as a whole.

Our Work

Advancing Science Through Action

At Sivasakthi Science Foundation, we are dedicated to supporting and advancing science research, training, and education through a variety of innovative programs and initiatives. We bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application.

Research Grants

We offer research grants to support cutting-edge scientific research in various fields. Our grants aim to provide researchers with the resources they need to explore new ideas, conduct experiments, and contribute to global scientific knowledge.

Educational Workshops

Our educational workshops are designed to provide hands-on learning experiences for students, educators, and researchers. These specialized workshops cover a wide range of biological and computational topics led by experts.

Fellowship Programs

Our fellowship programs support early-career scientists and researchers by providing crucial funding, mentorship, and networking opportunities. These programs are designed to help fellows develop their skills and accelerate their careers.

Community Outreach

We believe in the importance of engaging with the community to promote science education and literacy. Our community outreach programs include public lectures, accessible science fairs, and interactive exhibitions.

Online Learning

Our digital learning platform offers a range of courses and resources designed to make science education accessible to everyone. From introductory modules to advanced bioinformatics topics, we provide valuable learning opportunities for all.

Collaborative Projects

We partner with academic institutions, research organizations, and industry leaders to undertake collaborative projects that address pressing scientific challenges. These projects foster innovation and drive progress across disciplines.

Student Programs

We are committed to nurturing the next generation of scientists. Our student programs include structured internships, summer schools, and deep mentorship opportunities designed to inspire students interested in STEM.

How to Get Involved

If you are interested in participating in any of our programs, accessing our educational resources, or partnering on a new initiative, we would love to hear from you.
