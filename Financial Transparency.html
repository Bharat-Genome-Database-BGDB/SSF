Our Commitment

Accountability by Design

At Sivasakthi Science Foundation, we believe that the pursuit of scientific excellence must be matched by a commitment to financial honesty. Every resource entrusted to us—whether from donors, partners, or governmental grants—is treated as a sacred catalyst for discovery and education.
