Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Sivasakthi Science Foundation. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to ensure its protection.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you sign up for newsletters, make donations, or register for events.
  • Non-Personal Information: Browser type, IP address, and browsing behavior collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Communication: To send you updates, newsletters, and information about our programs and events.
  • Donations: To process your donations and send you acknowledgments and receipts.
  • Improvement: To analyze website usage and improve our services and user experience.
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and interests.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, or when required by law.

Data Security & Your Rights

We implement a variety of security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption, access controls, and secure servers.

You have the right to access, review, and request corrections to your personal information. You may also opt-out of receiving communications from us or request the deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@sivasakthifoundation.org.
