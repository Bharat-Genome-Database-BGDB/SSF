The Journey

A Collective Vision for Change

Established in 2023, the Sivasakthi Science Foundation was born from a pivotal realization: the gap between emerging scientific innovation and accessible education needed a bridge Our founders—a group of researchers and educators—aimed to create a platform that doesn't just fund research, but cultivates the minds capable of conducting it

Excellence

Maintaining rigorous standards in every pilot program and grant we issue

Collaboration

Active partnerships with global academic and research institutions

Inclusion

Ensuring science remains a tool for everyone, regardless of their background

Sustainability

Focusing on long-term ecological and societal benefits

Defining Moments

Key Milestones & Achievements

GenAi Labs

Academic Alliances

Forged strategic research and curriculum collaborations with VIT and Amrita University

Global Dialogue

Presented sustainable genomics resources at the Plant and Animal Genome (PAG) conference

Looking Ahead

Continuing the Legacy

From our early days offering scholarships to students in underrepresented communities to our current large-scale bioinformatics initiatives, our mission remains fixed We are scaling our research support and community engagement programs to ensure the scientists of tomorrow are ready for the challenges of today
