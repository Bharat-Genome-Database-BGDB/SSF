AI in Life Sciences

This focus area brings together algorithmic insight, biological understanding, and research infrastructure to support genome interpretation, molecular discovery, systems-level modeling, and emerging translational applications. The aim is not merely automation, but higher precision, deeper understanding, and better decisions across the life sciences workflow.

Research Themes

Transforming genome annotation

Deep learning enables higher-resolution interpretation of genomic structure, function, and variation across increasingly complex biological datasets.

Gene Prediction

Deep learning algorithms can identify gene locations and likely biological roles with increased automation, supporting the discovery of coding regions and previously under-characterized genomic elements.

Variant Detection

AI models help detect SNPs, insertions, deletions, and structural variation while also supporting downstream interpretation of how those changes may influence gene function or disease relevance.

Expression Analysis

By learning from complex transcriptomic patterns, deep learning can reveal regulatory relationships, response signatures, and biologically meaningful expression programs across conditions.

Evolutionary Genomics

Computational models can support the study of genome evolution, helping researchers identify conserved regions, predict divergence patterns, and better understand long-term biological change.

Personalized Medicine

When genomic features are integrated with wider biological data, AI can contribute to patient-specific prediction frameworks that support tailored therapeutic and diagnostic decisions.

Biological Systems

AI-driven insights into living systems

Beyond sequence interpretation, AI can help model adaptive biological behavior across cells, tissues, organisms, and ecosystems.

Neural Plasticity

AI can help model how neural pathways reorganize in response to learning, memory formation, and environmental change, expanding our understanding of adaptive brain function.

Cellular Plasticity

Computational approaches can decode how cells alter gene expression, regulatory state, and phenotype across development, stress, disease progression, and therapeutic response.

Ecosystem Adaptation

AI methods can support ecological analysis by identifying how species interactions and system-level responses shift under environmental change and selective pressure.

Developmental Biology

Predictive models can help study how organisms alter developmental trajectories under internal and external cues, improving our understanding of growth and differentiation.

Tissue Engineering

AI can assist regenerative medicine by modeling tissue behavior, guiding design choices, and supporting better control of adaptive responses during engineering workflows.
